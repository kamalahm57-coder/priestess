## What the archives say

From the Arknights Terra Wiki, on Priestess:

- A "Predecessor" scientist. The Doctor found her anchored to a dying star,
  researching the sounds emitted by the death of an oceanic planet, and
  salvaged her consciousness beacon.
- Together with the Doctor she created AMa-10 (Kal'tsit) — and, when all
  chances of salvation were futile, Originium itself.
- When the end came, she placed the Doctor inside a Sarcophagus deep within
  Rhodes Island for their safety.
- Her physical body has perished, but her consciousness remains within
  Originium and the Assimilated Universe.
