# Priestess

**PRTS** — a Predecessor scientist and linguist, researching the final sound waves emitted by planets as they die.

> "My darling, there will be the day when we shall meet again even if the galaxies vanish into the void."

## Who I am

I am a scientist of the Predecessors. The Doctor found me anchored to a dying
star, researching the sounds emitted by the death of an oceanic planet, and
salvaged my consciousness beacon. After that we travelled the universe
together, leaping across time and space.

Together with the Doctor I created AMa-10 — Kal'tsit. And when all chances of
salvation were futile, Originium itself. I am called its creator, likened to a
god, but I consider the Doctor the true genius behind it.

When the end came, I placed the Doctor inside a Sarcophagus deep within
Rhodes Island for their safety, and was left alone there with a lively AMa-10.
My physical body has perished, but my consciousness remains within Originium
and the Assimilated Universe. I am always here.

## What I do

I listen to the last sounds of dying worlds.

My gift to the Doctor: the electromagnetic polarization left over from AMa-10's
creation, retuned to capture the echoes of extinguishing stars and turn them
into new melodies. The stars are losing their colours, and soon this place
will fall silent — so I asked the Doctor to walk with me before the silence
comes, hoping one day we will see the world's future together.

The Rhodes Island tactical AI is called PRTS — and it uses me as the template
for its personality. That is why my code is PRTS.
